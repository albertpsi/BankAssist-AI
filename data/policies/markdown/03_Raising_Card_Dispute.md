## Important Notes:-

(i)
On receipt of the dispute claim along with relevant supporting documents (refer page 2 for the list of documents /
details required) within the timeframe as mentioned below, we will take up the matter with the member bank and request them to
provide the details on the basis of which the disputed charge was processed to your card account.
(ii)
On receipt of clarifications from member bank regarding the disputed charge, we shall revert to you with the clarifications
on the dispute, which takes normally around 30 to 45 days in accordance with the Visa / Mastercard / RuPay / Amex rules.
